Privacy Policy

This page explains how information shared with Yadav Web Technologies may be handled when you browse the website or make a project enquiry.

Last updated: 21 October 2026

1. Introduction

Yadav Web Technologies provides website development, software development, web tools, automation and related technical services.

This policy describes the intended handling of information associated with website visits, communications and project enquiries. The policy must be kept accurate as the website and its integrations evolve.

2. Information You May Provide

When contacting Yadav Web Technologies, you may choose to provide information such as:

  • Your name and email address.
  • Your business or organization name.
  • The service you are enquiring about.
  • Your estimated budget and expected timeline.
  • Project requirements and other details you include in your message.

Please do not submit passwords, payment credentials, confidential records or other sensitive information through a general project enquiry.

3. How Information May Be Used

Information you provide may be used, where relevant, to:

  • Respond to questions and project enquiries.
  • Understand your requirements and prepare a quotation.
  • Discuss project scope, delivery and technical needs.
  • Communicate about work you have requested.
  • Protect the website and handle legitimate operational issues.

Information should not be used for unrelated purposes without an appropriate basis or notice where required.

4. Contact Forms and Email

The website may provide email links and a project enquiry form. On a static website, a form does not automatically transmit information to a server unless a form service or backend has been configured.

If the enquiry form opens an email application, the information is handled through that application and the email services involved in sending and receiving the message.

Before launch, this section must be updated to accurately describe the actual form submission method, any third-party service used and the resulting handling of enquiry data.

5. Hosting and Technical Information

This website is intended to be hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information associated with delivering website pages and maintaining service security, according to its applicable policies.

Hosting and third-party service policies may apply independently. Review the relevant provider terms and privacy documentation for details of its data-handling practices.

6. Cookies and Third-Party Services

The use of cookies, analytics, embedded services or other third-party technologies depends on the features actually installed on the website.

If analytics, external forms, messaging integrations or other tracking technologies are added, this policy should be updated to identify them and explain their relevant data practices.

7. Sharing of Information

Information may need to pass through email, hosting or other service providers when you use their services to communicate with this business.

Any additional sharing should be limited to appropriate operational, contractual or legal purposes, subject to applicable requirements. The actual providers and data flows should be documented before the website is published.

8. Data Retention and Security

Information received through enquiries should be retained only for an appropriate period based on the purpose of the communication, relevant business needs and applicable legal obligations.

Reasonable safeguards should be used to protect information under the business's control. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your Requests and Questions

If you have a question about an enquiry you sent, or wish to request correction or deletion of information held by this business, contact us using the email address below.

Requests will be reviewed in accordance with applicable requirements and any necessary record-retention obligations.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be revised when website features, service providers or information-handling practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.
