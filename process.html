PROJECT WORKFLOW

How a project moves forward

Each stage helps establish clear expectations before moving to the next step.

01

Requirement Discussion

Understand your business, intended users, project goals and the problem you want to solve.

02

Scope & Planning

Identify the required pages, features, integrations, technical needs and deliverables.

03

Quotation & Agreement

Review the proposed scope, pricing, delivery expectations and applicable conditions before work begins.

04

Design & Development

Build the website or software according to the agreed requirements and approved design.

05

Testing & Review

Review the implementation, check relevant functionality and address agreed corrections.

06

Delivery & Deployment

Complete the final review and arrange delivery, handover or deployment as agreed.

CLEAR EXPECTATIONS

What matters throughout the project

Good project outcomes depend on clear requirements, communication and agreed scope.

Defined Requirements

Required features and deliverables should be identified clearly before implementation.

Clear Communication

Important decisions, scope changes and project expectations should be communicated and agreed upon.

Relevant Testing

Testing should match the actual project, its features and its intended use.

PROJECT SCOPE

Handling additional requirements

Requirements can become clearer during a project. If a new feature or major change is requested, its effect on the agreed scope, cost and timeline should be reviewed before implementation.

Additional work should be started only after the necessary details and expectations are agreed upon.
